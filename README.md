# 🦔 Modern Sonic V6 (Steak-Fries-Build)
### A high-speed Modern Sonic experience for Sonic Robo Blast 2!

![SRB2](https://img.shields.io/badge/SRB2-Mod-blue)
![Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/Version-V6-red)
![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-In%20Development-success)

> ⚡ Go fast. Boost hard. Style it your way.

**Modern Sonic V6** is a customizable Modern Sonic character mod for **Sonic Robo Blast 2**, bringing together mechanics and inspiration from multiple Modern Sonic games.

From **Sonic Rush** to **Sonic Frontiers**, you can customize how Sonic moves, boosts, looks, sounds, and performs tricks!

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎨 Character Customization

Make Sonic look the way you want!

- 🔵 Change Sonic's character colors
- 🧤 Change glove colors
- 👟 Change shoe colors
- 🎨 Create your own color combinations

---

## 💨 Multiple Boost Styles

Choose from several boost styles inspired by different Modern Sonic games.

| Boost Style | Inspired By |
|---|---|
| 🏁 **Rush** | Sonic Rush |
| 🌌 **Frontiers** | Sonic Frontiers |
| 🔥 **Unleashed** | Sonic Unleashed |
| 💥 **Forces** | Sonic Forces |
| ⚡ **Generations** | Sonic Generations |
| 🌈 **Colors** | Sonic Colors |
| 🔥 **All Of Them** | Combines the available styles |

> Can't decide? Try **All Of Them**!

---

## 🌀 Movement Options

Choose how you want Sonic to move.

- 💨 Optional **Drop Dash**
- 🌀 Optional **Spin Dash**

Both can be enabled or disabled depending on your preferred playstyle.

---

## 🎯 Perfect Homing Attack

Perform a **Perfect Homing Attack** to gain access to stylish tricks.

Features include:

- 🎭 Trick animations
- 🐦 Flicky trick animations
- ⚡ Stylish aerial movement
- 💨 More ways to maintain momentum

> **Hit it perfectly. Keep the momentum going.**

---

## 🏃 Rush Mode

Want an even faster experience?

Enable **Rush Mode** for a gameplay style focused on speed, momentum, and fast-paced movement inspired by the *Sonic Rush* games.

---

## 🎙️ Voice Options

Modern Sonic V6 includes multiple voice options, allowing you to choose the voice that fits your preferred version of Modern Sonic.

---

## ⚙️ Customization

Modern Sonic V6 is built around player choice.

You can mix different options together to create your own Sonic setup.

### Example Setup

```text
Character Colors: Custom
Gloves: Custom
Shoes: Custom

Boost: Frontiers
Rush Mode: ON

Drop Dash: ON
Spin Dash: OFF

Perfect Homing: ON
Flicky Tricks: ON

Voice: Custom
